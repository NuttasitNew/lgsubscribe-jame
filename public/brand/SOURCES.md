# Brand asset sources

- `lg-logo.svg` — exact path geometry and colors from the official LG common logo asset: https://www.lg.com/content/dam/lge/common/logo/logo-lg-100-44.svg
- `lg-logo-social.png` — official Primary Logo image from LG Global Brand Identity: https://www.lg.com/content/dam/lge/global/our-brand/src/mocks/bs0002/brand-elements-logo-primary-d.png
- `app/icon.svg` — square crop of the official LG face mark from the same source for favicon and manifest use.

The logo geometry and colors must not be redrawn, recolored, or generated.
