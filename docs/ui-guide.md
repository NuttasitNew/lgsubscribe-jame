# Public UI reference

Use this page as the small index for the public site. The implementation remains the source of truth.

- Stack and tokens: `app/globals.css` defines colors, type, `container-page`, `section-space`, `eyebrow`, and `page-hero`. Check the relevant Next.js guide in `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before editing framework behavior.
- Public shell: `app/(public)/layout.tsx` composes `components/site-header.tsx`, `components/site-footer.tsx`, `components/floating-line-contact.tsx`, and `components/mobile-dock.tsx`.
- Promotion popup: `components/floating-subscribe-day.tsx` uses the same Radix Dialog primitive as `components/ui/sheet.tsx`, with keyboard focus, Escape/backdrop close, and per-session dismissal. `lib/subscribe-day.ts` schedules the portrait Subscribe Day "ฉลองครบรอบ 2 ปี ยิ่งซับ ยิ่งฟิน ลุ้นบินมัลดีฟส์" artwork (ลด 15%) for 21–29 November 2026 in Asia/Bangkok; the module keeps its Subscribe Day name as "the current promotion". It opens only after the browser checks the current time and fits both viewport width and height; static HTML stays closed.
- Promotion page: `/subscribe-anniversary/` (the old `/subscribe-day/` forwards there with its query while the campaign runs, otherwise to `/`) in `feature/public/subscribe-day/components/subscribe-day-page.tsx` uses a tropical palette (sky/lagoon, sand, gold frames, red ribbons, SVG hibiscus/plumeria/palm in `tropical-decor.tsx`) limited to this page, matching the "ลุ้นบินมัลดีฟส์" poster (offer cards copy its red pill + red-bordered peach card, existing customers first); the Maldives draw is shown only as LG's headline with "เงื่อนไขเป็นไปตามที่บริษัทฯ กำหนด" (no invented rules). It checks `isSubscribeDayActive` per request, redirects to `/` outside the campaign window, is `noindex`, stays out of the sitemap, and suppresses the popup on its own route and the old path. `subscribe-day-products.ts` picks the recommended products: the `kw` query (Google Ads `{keyword}`) selects a category, otherwise one product per leading category. Cards use the promotion artwork, the first-bill price less the campaign discount, the regular price from the current price list, and omit view/order counters. The review section reuses the verified WT1410NHEG photos.
- Primitives: `components/ui/button.tsx` for actions, `components/ui/badge.tsx` for labels, `components/ui/card.tsx` for framed content. Example: `feature/public/products/components/product-detail-page.tsx`.
- Backoffice: `/backoffice/analytics/` in `app/(backoffice)/backoffice/(protected)/analytics/page.tsx` uses a light gray page, white sections, compact Thai shadcn date/month pickers and shared `Button`. Tables own their border and surface. Desktop/mobile filters wrap; charts/tables scroll within their section. `/backoffice/auth/login/` uses `feature/backoffice/components/backoffice-login.tsx`. Private pages belong in `(protected)` and check the session again before reading data. Analytics was checked locally at desktop (1280px) and mobile (390px), including login/logout, month shortcuts, date filtering and keyboard focus; public marketing colors/layout remain defined by the public shell.
- Source/Google tables use `feature/backoffice/components/report-table.tsx` for the shared heading and surface. Below 640px each row becomes a labeled vertical summary; desktop keeps the table. Daily visits and top pages use the same component. All metrics remain visible on phones without table-wide horizontal scrolling. `traffic-breakdown.tsx` separates website source/campaign/device signals; `google-reports.tsx` separates Ads, GA4 and sync status. Missing Google snapshots show a waiting state rather than invented zeros. Public consent uses `components/tracking-consent.tsx` and shared `Button`, below the existing promotional dialog (z80/81) and above the mobile dock. Cookie choices can be reopened from the fixed small button.
- Product details: `feature/public/products/components/product-detail-page.tsx` owns the section order; `product-gallery.tsx` handles product image navigation; `product-specifications.tsx` displays verified specifications. Route: `/products/[slug]/`.
- WashTower reviews: `feature/public/products/components/wash-tower-reviews.tsx` shows four photos of WT1410NHEG, verified by a readable model code on its carton. WT1410NHEN shows a customer photo matching its navy/beige finish plus two WashTower carton delivery photos that do not show a machine. WT2520NHEN is withdrawn from the October sales catalog. Do not label these three photos as an exact model or a single installation job. Other albums remain unpublished. Images load lazily. Do not present photographs as written customer testimonials.
- Monthly sales availability: `lib/oct-subscription-campaign.ts` uses the October workbook in `data/Price list_Oct.xlsx`; `lib/catalog-products.ts` publishes only matched current models. `lib/promotion-image-assets.ts` maps 67 reviewed October artworks to 68 products, including the shared WD516AN/WD518AN image. Withdrawn, paused, Control Stock and unmatched models have no sales detail route or sitemap entry. Knowledge guides and historical specifications are retained separately.
- Catalog search: `feature/public/products/components/product-catalog-browser.tsx` keeps query and category in the URL; `components/product-card.tsx` carries those filters to the detail route, and `feature/public/products/components/product-catalog-back-link.tsx` restores the filtered listing.
- Representative public pages: `feature/public/home/components/home-page.tsx` shows the homepage review and product cards; `feature/public/what-is-lg-subscribe/components/what-is-lg-subscribe-page.tsx` shows guide sections. These references were source-inspected for this guide.

Keep section content within `container-page`, use existing responsive spacing and red calls to action, and check desktop and mobile after visual changes. The public marketing site has its own warm neutral and red palette; backoffice defaults do not apply here.

Historical views: Vercel history is the primary traffic chart/table; first-party details collapse beneath it. For periods before first-party collection started, website totals use “—” and the empty daily list/chart is omitted; Google history has its own menu page. Mobile rows use labeled summaries below 640px.

Calendar controls: `components/ui/calendar.tsx` and `popover.tsx` use the shadcn New York/Radix registry; `thai-calendar.tsx` supplies Thai weekday/month names, Buddhist years, Thai screen-reader labels, and shared shadcn Select dropdowns. `thai-date-picker.tsx` provides shared date/month fields, and `feature/backoffice/components/analytics-date-filters.tsx` uses them for every analytics filter. Civil dates stay Gregorian ISO in submitted queries and use Asia/Bangkok for selection/display.

Backoffice navigation: `feature/backoffice/components/backoffice-shell.tsx` is the authenticated shared shell: desktop sidebar, sticky header, shadcn Sheet mobile menu, active route, identity and logout. LINE pages require the database user’s `line.view` permission. LINE uses real section anchors instead of placeholder menu items. Thai calendars use a white surface explicitly because portal content inherits public theme tokens.

Responsive review (development): login, analytics and LINE checked at 320, 390, 768 and 1280px. No page-level horizontal overflow in the rendered data/empty states; LINE free-text fields wrap long messages and identifiers. Section anchors leave space for the sticky header.

Public mobile review: all eight static/public pages and all 68 published product detail routes checked at 320px. Product detail single-column grids use `grid-cols-1` (minmax(0,1fr)); model/title text can wrap and the price row wraps. `feature/public/authorized/components/authorized-page.tsx` uses a constrained single-column profile grid and smaller mobile agent code. Both fixes preserve desktop layouts and were visually checked with gallery navigation.

Login password field: shared Button eye toggle with Thai accessible labels, pressed state and 44px touch target; password stays masked initially. Signed session and cookie last 24 hours from login.

Backoffice menu and permissions: `lib/backoffice/permissions.ts` is the canonical menu/permission registry. The shell groups reports, LINE and user administration and contains only backoffice links. `line-directory.tsx` shares search, pagination and responsive ReportTable for LINE users/messages; `users-manager.tsx` provides creation and permission editing. `line-settings.tsx` (`/backoffice/line/settings/`, `line.manage`) is write-only: it shows status, last four characters and who saved each LINE secret, never the value. Shared `components/ui/password-input.tsx` supplies the eye toggle for login and user passwords. Google reports have their own `/backoffice/google/` route and pass the form action to the existing Thai date filters. Pages and API handlers verify permissions before reading/writing data; hidden menus alone are not access control.

Date filters: one desktop row with monthly selection/apply on the left and start/end/apply on the right. Six recent-month shortcuts live inside the month popover. At narrow widths, the same shared controls stack and the range uses two columns with a full-width submit button. Both website and Google reports reuse this layout.

Vercel history: `vercel-traffic.tsx` reads persisted UTC daily aggregates and exact period totals from `lib/analytics/vercel-report.ts`. Import once with `node scripts/import-vercel-traffic.mjs development|production [start] [end]` after migrating the intended database. The script uses the existing Vercel CLI login, validates full query bounds before an atomic import, and never uploads a Vercel token. It caches monthly, full-import-range, today, trailing 7/30-day unique totals. Other custom ranges retain their daily table/pageviews; unavailable exact unique totals show “—” with a tooltip instead of a sum. Selected collection/attribution explanation paragraphs live in code comments; no duplicate source navigation or date-summary paragraph is rendered.

Staff price list: `/price-list/` (`app/(staff)/price-list/page.tsx`, `feature/staff/price-list/components/`) is a staff tool outside the public shell — public for now, `noindex`, disallowed in robots and absent from the sitemap; it will move behind backoffice login. Only Subscription options are shown (outright/ซื้อขาด rows are filtered out in `page.tsx`). Data comes from `data/price-list/current.json`, produced by `pnpm import:price-list "<Price list PDF>"` (`scripts/import-price-list.py`, categories detected from page headings). `lib/price-list.ts` builds the installment table from the PDF bill ranges (gaps at the regular fee, advance payment added on top as in the PDF worked example). The page is display-first: the chosen product fills the screen (from 1024px summary left, schedule right) and products are picked in a dialog opened from the header search button (`price-list-picker.tsx`: row 1 search, row 2 category chips, then the product list; full screen on phones, tall centred panel from 768px, list in 2 columns; Enter picks the first hit; opens automatically when no `?model=`). Units are added/removed from one control (`cart-controls.tsx` `QuantityStepper`: "+ เพิ่ม" then "− n +") in both the dialog rows (adds the model's first Subscription contract) and the product page (adds the chosen contract); `SelectedBar` shows the total units and one entry per chosen contract option (horizontal chips under the header, `layout="list"` stacked rows capped at 30dvh in the dialog) with its quantity (− n +; − at 1 becomes ✕ and removes it) plus "ดูยอดรวม". The schedule panel switches between "ภาพรวม (กล่องรายเดือน)" (default: one box per month, columns are bills 1–12 with each row labelled by year and bill range, full baht amounts rounded to whole baht — never abbreviated like 1.8k; the legend keeps the exact satang, promo tiers coloured, average per month on top; on phones 6 boxes per line, each labelled "งวด N", amounts always visible) and "ตาราง" (yearly/range/monthly). Search is full-text (`searchModels`: every word must match model codes, details incl. BTU, contracts, promotions or prices; punctuation ignored; everyday synonyms like แอร์/ทีวี; model-code hits ranked first). Regular prices show struck through next to promo/average figures. Product photos are cropped from the PDF into `public/images/price-list/`. The stepper builds a multi-unit order ("ดูยอดรวม" → cart view) that sums monthly bills and applies the PDF combo discount (`combos` in the JSON; new customer ≥2 Subscription units, existing ≥1; % off each bill after the single promo). The page opens with `customer-profile.tsx` `CustomerChooser` (ลูกค้าใหม่/ลูกค้าเดิม, cannot be dismissed until chosen, kept in `?customer=`); the header's left `CustomerBadge` (avatar chip) reopens it, and the chosen type drives the combo rule on the product page and cart (no toggle inside the cart). Savings are shown in a solid green `SavingsBanner` (total plus per-promo pills) on the product page and cart, and cart items carry a green "−N% ทุกบิล" pill. A "ล้างรายการ" button (cart header, header bar, dialog list; asks to confirm) empties the order. The cart view shows one card per contract option with its quantity stepper and an eye "ตาราง" button that opens that unit's own schedule (combo applied) in a dialog (85dvh floating panel on phones) (the header bar is hidden there); with 2+ different Subscription products its schedule panel switches between "รวมทุกเครื่อง" (combined) and "แยกรายเครื่อง" (one timeline per product, per unit). The dialog ends with a sticky "เลือกเสร็จ · ดูราคารวม N เครื่อง" button that closes it and opens the cart view. `promo-bar.tsx` sits above the product/cart view: switches for "โปรรายรุ่น" (PDF bill ranges; off = every bill at the regular fee via `buildInstallmentSchedule(item, { promo: false })`) and the best combo running today for the chosen customer type — locked off (dashed amber, "เพิ่มอีก N เครื่อง") until the order's Subscription units meet its rule, switchable once they do — with an amber warning while any is off. Combos merge the PDF pages with the banner campaign (`subscribeDayCombos` in `lib/subscribe-day.ts`, 15% for 21–29 Nov 2026, new ≥2 / existing ≥1); when they overlap the larger percent wins (`findCombo`). The product page previews this contract's first bill and average with the combo.
